How the scores work
Financial Health (FH)
  • Revenue size and growth trajectory
  • Wage-to-revenue ratio
  • Transfer market profitability over 3 years
  • Debt levels relative to revenue
  • Commercial revenue diversity — matchday, broadcast, sponsorship
  • Ownership financial strength and commitment
Investment Attractiveness (IA)
  • Managerial stability and coaching quality
  • Squad age profile and future sell-on value
  • Stadium situation and infrastructure upside
  • League and market growth trajectory
  • Brand strength in underpenetrated markets
  • World Cup 2026 exposure for North American clubs

Scores are generated using publicly available financial data — Deloitte Football Money League 2026, club annual accounts, and UEFA Financial Sustainability reports — and AI analysis by Anthropic / Claude. This index is a framework for discussion, not financial advice.
